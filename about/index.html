About

Lead product designer, casino vertical at Novibet

I started in art direction, then moved into product design. What carried over is the eye for craft. What's changed is the approach: more systems thinking, more ownership, less one-off screens.

I lead product design for Novibet's casino vertical, working closely with product and engineering to take ideas from problem to shipped feature. Part of the role is pushing for more process discipline on the team: clearer intake, clearer documentation, decisions that hold up when someone asks why later.

I care about the reasoning behind a decision as much as the decision itself: what was tested, what was assumed, and what I'd want to check given more time. That's the bar I hold my own work to, whether it ships clean or not.

Key skills

Product thinking

Starting from the problem and the player, not the screen.

Interaction design

How something behaves, not just how it looks: states, transitions, edge cases.

Information architecture

Structuring content and navigation so complexity stays easy to scan.

Systems thinking

Designing configuration, not one-off screens: order attributes, sort rules, and shared components that hold up across a dozen providers.

Prototyping

Figma-first, from low-fidelity flows through to production-ready, developer-handoff specs.

Visual design

Pixel-perfect UI, type and color systems, motion and micro-interaction detail.

Outcome thinking

Judging a decision by what it changed, not just that it shipped.

Cross-functional collaboration

Working directly with engineering and product in an Agile environment, including handoff specs precise enough to implement without a design re-review per change.
